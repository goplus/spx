basic-chinese.ttf is a modified glyph subset of Source Han Sans CN Medium 1.000.

Original font copyright (c) 2014 Adobe Systems Incorporated.
Source: https://github.com/goplus/godot/releases/download/spx2.0.14/CnFont.ttf
Upstream source: https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-sans/tree/1.000

Modification: retained Han ideographs, CJK radicals and strokes, Chinese and
fullwidth punctuation, and compatibility ideographs; removed Latin glyphs.
